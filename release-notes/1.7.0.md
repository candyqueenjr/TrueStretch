## TrueStretch 1.7.0 — See if Optimize Max really gave you more FPS

### What's new
- **Before vs after Optimize Max.** The "Your last match" row (under Your PC) now also compares your real average FPS in the matches you played after your first Optimize Max change with the matches you played before it. For example: "Since Optimize Max (14 Nov): 210 FPS on average over 3 matches, before: 195 FPS over 5 matches (+8%)."
- The numbers come from VALORANT's own statistics, which the game writes into its log at the end of every round. TrueStretch only reads them. Nothing is changed and nothing is sent anywhere.

### Why it matters for the player
Lots of "FPS boost" tools promise big numbers and never show you what really happened. This shows your real result from your own matches, so you can see whether the changes paid off on your PC.

### Details
- TrueStretch uses the 5 matches closest to the change on each side, and at least 5 minutes of play on each side. A match that was already running when you applied is left out.
- Different maps and modes change FPS too. The comparison gets more accurate with every match you play.
- It only appears when there are matches from both before and after in the logs VALORANT keeps, and it is skipped when your in-game FPS limit sets the FPS (then both numbers are just the limit). If you undo every change, it goes away.
- Also checked for this release: VALORANT 13.10. Every game-log line TrueStretch relies on still looks the same.
