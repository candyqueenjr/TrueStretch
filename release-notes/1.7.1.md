## TrueStretch 1.7.1 — Memory full? See whether it's VALORANT or your other programs

### What's new
- **Who used up your memory.** When your PC's memory was 90% full or more during your last match, the "Your last match" row (under Your PC) now says how much of it VALORANT itself used and how much your other programs used. For example: "Windows memory was 97% full during the match: VALORANT itself used 2.0 GB of your 16 GB, other programs about 14 GB."
- The advice follows from that. If other programs used most of it, closing browsers, launchers and other apps frees the most. If the game itself needs most of it, closing apps helps only a little and more memory (RAM) helps most.
- The numbers come from VALORANT's own statistics, which the game writes into its log at the end of every round. TrueStretch only reads them. Nothing is changed and nothing is sent anywhere.

### Why it matters for the player
When memory runs full in a match you get stutters and hitches. "Close your background apps" is common advice, but it only helps if those apps are what's filling the memory. Now you can see which it is on your own PC.

### Details
- The numbers come from the round where memory was fullest. Your PC's total memory is worked out from VALORANT's own figures.
- Older game logs that don't have these lines still show the short advice from before.
- Also checked for this release: VALORANT 13.10. Every game-log line TrueStretch relies on still looks the same.
