## TrueStretch 2.0.0 — Redesigned, safer, and now shows how every match really ran

The biggest update so far: a full check of every screen, the code underneath, and what players asked for.

### What's new
- **Matches page + a report after every match.** When a match ends, TrueStretch tells you how it ran: map, average FPS, slow frames, game delay and what held the FPS back (graphics card, processor or your FPS limit), straight from VALORANT's own statistics. The new **Matches** page keeps your whole history (VALORANT deletes its own logs after a few days), shows an FPS chart of your recent matches, and tells you what changed your FPS: before vs after Optimize Max, and by stretch size. You can turn the report off in Settings.
- **Troubleshooting window.** Black bars, no stretch, hotkeys not working: the common problems and their fixes, one click away on the VALORANT page and in Settings. It opens by itself when a Test stretch didn't fill your screen.

### What's better
- **VALORANT page redesigned.** The stretch sizes are now right at the top instead of below the fold. When everything is fine the checklist shrinks to one line ("All 6 checks passed"), and it opens by itself when something needs you. Less repeated information.
- **Accounts you can name.** A Rename button instead of a hidden double-click, and the list no longer jumps or cancels your typing while you rename.
- **Optimize Max is clearer.** It opens on what actually needs doing ("To do"). One **Optimize** button instead of an Apply button on every row, your choices stay ticked when you switch filters, and the technical report opens in its own window.
- **Cleaner look.** Red is now only used for the main actions. Switches and checkboxes are calmer, danger buttons stand out, faint text is easier to read, and the light theme looks right (theme changes now apply at once, no restart).
- **The window fits your screen.** It no longer opens taller than a small laptop screen.
- **Keyboard friendly.** You can see where you are when you use Tab, and screen readers announce every switch and tile by name.
- **PLAY shows what's happening.** It says "Starting…" while VALORANT launches and can't be pressed twice. If VALORANT isn't installed, the first-run tour says so (with a Get VALORANT button) instead of "You're all set".
- **Test stretch fixed.** Waiting for the countdown no longer counts as "black bars". Enter now means "yes, it fills the screen".
- **Fewer pop-ups.** An error now shows once, in one place.

### What's fixed (safety)
- **A crash or power cut mid-match can no longer leave your PC stretched after a restart.** Windows was saving the stretched resolution while the picture scaling was set during a match. It isn't saved any more.
- **Switching back can't fail silently any more.** If Windows refuses or the screen doesn't answer (driver reset, monitor reconnecting), TrueStretch keeps trying every few seconds, never mid-match, and falls back to Windows' saved resolution. It only forgets the stretch once your screen has really left it.
- **"Stay stretched when TrueStretch quits" is respected.** The background guard no longer switches back anyway.
- **No more stacks of error messages over the game.** An unexpected error is logged and shown once, never every half second.
- **Files can't be half-written.** Settings, the undo list for Optimize Max, the crash-recovery record, VALORANT's settings files and restored backups are now written crash-safely. A damaged file is set aside instead of silently wiping your undo history, and a settings file with characters TrueStretch can't read safely is left untouched.
- **Undo tells you what it couldn't put back** instead of quietly skipping it, and never edits VALORANT's files while the game is running.
- Smaller fixes:
  - VALORANT no longer briefly shows as "not installed" while it renames its logs.
  - A custom resolution dropped by a driver update is re-added instead of failing until restart.
  - The refresh-rate fix is no longer offered while the desktop is stretched.
  - The log file can rotate again.

Also checked for this release: VALORANT 13.10. The game-log lines TrueStretch relies on are unchanged.
