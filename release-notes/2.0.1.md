## TrueStretch 2.0.1 — Every screen checked at small and large window sizes, in both themes

A design pass over every page, tab and dialog of 2.0.0, in dark and light theme, on a big window and on a small
laptop window. These are the things that looked wrong or confusing, fixed.

### What's better
- **Problems read as problems.** When a check fails, the VALORANT page used to say "VALORANT installed" next to a warning icon. A failing check now says what's wrong: "VALORANT not found", "Riot Client not found", "Monitor drivers still on", "No account ticked". A blocking problem gets a red cross, a warning a yellow triangle.
- **"Get VALORANT" right where it's needed.** If VALORANT isn't on this PC, the VALORANT page offers the official download next to the problem instead of only saying "not found". The page also no longer claims a game version for a game that isn't installed, and the first-run tour no longer lists "VALORANT wasn't found" twice.
- **Small windows: the tabs keep their names.** At under 1000 px the top navigation used to turn four of the five tabs into bare icons while "VALORANT" kept its word. All five now keep their labels, set a little tighter, at every window size. Growing the window back no longer leaves the labels cut off.
- **Optimize Max fits a small window.** The score card used to stack the score, the counts and the buttons into a 320-pixel tower on a 900-pixel-wide window. The score and the counts now stay side by side and only the buttons move down (232 px).
- **Settings tabs are centred** over the settings pages, like the game's own settings menu, instead of hugging the left edge while the pages sat in the middle.
- **Matches: the four summary cards line up.** One card with a two-line caption used to push its neighbours' captions out of line.
- **My PC: screen rows in plain words.** "scaling: none (identity) · monitor driver disabled" is now "Windows scaling: none · monitor driver off (good for stretched res) · HDMI · on the NVIDIA GeForce …".

### What's fixed
- **My PC could open half-empty.** Opening the page sometimes showed an empty "Your screens" card and the health checks stretched into tall empty boxes until you resized the window: the rows were added but never shown. They are now shown right away, on every page. And if reading the screens ever fails (a display reconnecting, a driver reset), the card says so in one line and the rest of the page still appears.
