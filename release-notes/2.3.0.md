## TrueStretch 2.3.0 — Game Color: vivid colours in VALORANT, only while you play

A new **Color** tab on the VALORANT page. Many players turn up *Digital vibrance* (NVIDIA) or *Saturation* (AMD)
so enemies and abilities pop — and then live with an over-coloured desktop, or keep switching it by hand.
TrueStretch now does it for you, only while the game is in front.

### What's new
- **Game Color.** Switch on *Vivid colours in VALORANT* and pick a strength: **Light, Medium, Strong or Max**. The
  colours turn up the moment VALORANT is the window in front and go back to normal as soon as you alt-tab, close the
  game or quit TrueStretch. Your desktop, videos and Discord always look normal.
- **See it before you play.** A before/after colour strip shows the difference at the strength you picked, and
  **Preview 4 s** shows it for real on your desktop for four seconds.
- **Your own setting is kept.** The tab shows your screen's normal level and the in-game level (for example
  *NVIDIA digital vibrance — normal 50 %, in VALORANT 75 %*). If you already used your own vibrance, that exact value
  comes back afterwards.
- **Never stuck colourful.** The level from before is saved first, so if TrueStretch crashes, is closed from Task
  Manager or the PC loses power, the colours are put back automatically (by the guard process, or at the next start).
- **Works with NVIDIA and AMD graphics.** If your screen runs on graphics without this setting (many laptops' built-in
  screens run on the Intel chip), the tab tells you so instead of doing nothing.

### What's fixed
- **The Accounts, Display and Launch tabs now show fresh information when you open them.** They used to keep what they
  had at the last change until something else happened (for example, the Display tab could show an old desktop size).

### What's better
- The website's FAQ now says exactly what TrueStretch connects to (the update check and Riot's public status page) and
  answers whether Game Color is allowed.
